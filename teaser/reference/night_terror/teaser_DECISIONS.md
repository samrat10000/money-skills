# Teaser 1: decisions and why
- Hero monster = the reindeer STALKER, not the Brute. Sam: "the reindeer one" is the main. The Brute only gets a 4-frame lightning silhouette (907).
- No text on screen, no shots reused from Ep1-3, 19 s, ending on the exact poster frame (POSTER_CAM), all at Sam's request.
- Score: cut from the locked Ep1 music v5 (score_cut.py -> score_cut_v1.wav), because Sam hated AI music. Real recorded SFX are still pending: the free SFX sites block the cloud, and Flow Music needs Sam signed in to Chrome.
- Fog off for every cut (NOFOG) for speed and consistency. The hero frames were checked side by side with the poster first.
- 910 eye close-up: shoot one eye side-on. The Stalker's eyes sit on the sides of the skull, so aiming at the midpoint between them showed only bone.
- Skull v3 = a dead BULL/cow skull (Sam 2026-11-22). Keep the antlers. The scary "wrongness" comes from blade teeth where cattle have none, roots showing through eroded bone, a cracked forehead and deep ember eyes. This skull is reused in later episodes.
- Shots that show the new skull (909, 910, 912) render in the cloud. The Asus keeps 902-908 and then 911, because 911 is long and must match the PC look.
- Naming: every clip is NUMBER_short_name (Sam liked the numbers).
